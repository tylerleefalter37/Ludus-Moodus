I. Choose How They Die: Mechanics & Harvest Integrity

The mechanism of execution directly dictates what drops into the Spoliarium morgue:

  • Spiked Portcullis: Kinetic fall delivers high puncturing trauma. Leaves facial masks 100% pristine if calibrated to chest height.
  • Numidian Lion Cage: Starved beast release shreds the victim. Leaves claws and pelts, but mangles blood and soft tissue.
  • Hegmata Pitfall: Counterbalanced floor drop into soft sand trenches. Leaves vascular system intact for instant arterial tapping.
  • Naumachia Sluice Gate: Aqueduct water drowns the victim with crocs. Preserves unbruised gladiator dermis and lion skins.
  • Scythed Post: Torsion spring rotation severs limbs. Yields dismembered trophies and teeth.

II. Historical Roman Commodities & Plinian Medicine

Roman medical and cosmetic texts record extraordinary values for gladiator byproducts:

  • Warm Arterial Blood: Pliny the Elder and Celsus documented that physicians prescribed warm gladiator blood straight from a severed neck to cure the comitialis morbus ("falling sickness" / epilepsy). Must be bottled before coagulating.
  • Gladiator Sweat (*Strigilmentum*): Scraped from muscle with iron strigils. Sold to Roman matrons as an elite skin ointment and aphrodisiac.
  • Arena Sand (*Harena*): Blood-drenched sand scooped from the kill perimeter, carried by chariot race gamblers as good luck charms.
